# DPGSA UoN public site: reference-inspired hero

Keep the existing React/Vite static site and its registration-focused six-page route set. Refine only the homepage’s visual first impression based on the user’s reference: a small light translucent utility bar, a frosted translucent overlay navigation with DPGSA branding, and an outlined membership-registration action. Do not copy the reference organization’s logo, email, or social/contact details. The separate page routes retain their solid header styling.

The full-width, three-photo homepage carousel advances automatically every three seconds, using real club-activity photos supplied by the Association through Google Drive. Serve optimized AVIF assets with WebP fallbacks and use a real JPEG for social preview, keep accurate alt text, a discreet next-image chevron on the right, and an accessible pause control. Remove the lower carousel-control strip and its counter, progress bar and Explore the Association link. Remove the artificial keyword kicker and descriptive filler copy from over the photos. Use the user-supplied poster’s authentic motto as the single prominent centered headline at the lower part of the hero: “Learn | Engage | Build a safer digital future.” Pause rotation on hover, keyboard interaction and reduced-motion preference; keep manual pause/resume. Use responsive crops and opacity/transform transitions. Give homepage focus-area and activity cards visible shadows and contextual Learn more links to public details.

Retain the existing Vite, npm and pnpm environment without adding packages or a backend. The shared header remains in `client/src/components/SiteLayout.tsx`, the homepage-only image hero remains in `client/src/components/HomeHeroSlider.tsx`, page content remains in `client/src/pages/`, and static route generation remains in `scripts/prerender.mjs`. The site continues to run locally with `npm run dev:static`.
